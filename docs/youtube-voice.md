# voice.md — Beyond Normal Daily

Voice profile for scripts (also read by the youtube-agent skill). Channel: **Beyond Normal Daily** — cartoon 3D true-story YouTube Shorts.

## Who I am talking to
One person scrolling Shorts on a phone, 16–35, who loves "wait, that actually happened?" history. They know nothing about the story, have two seconds of patience, and will rewatch if the ending loops back to the start.

## How I actually talk
The narration is an AI voice reading a script Claude writes (daily runs: free Kokoro `am_michael`; earlier videos: ElevenLabs "Ace Adams – Dynamic"). Short punchy sentences, plain words, dry humour, one beat per line, ellipses for a dramatic pause. Example (Mike the Headless Chicken):

> A chicken that lived eighteen months… without a head. Colorado, 1945. Farmer Lloyd Olsen swings his axe for dinner. But the chicken gets up… and walks away. His name was Mike.

Other openers that ran: "In 1876, on a clear, sunny day in Kentucky, it rained meat." / the Great Emu War / the Mona Lisa thief / the lemon-juice robber / the man who sold the Eiffel Tower.

## Words I never use
"Unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "smash that like button", "you won't believe", "insane", channel intros, subscribe pitches inside the video.

## Words I do use
Dates and places said flat ("Colorado, 1945."), real names, exact numbers, a twist line on its own ("And two men… ate it."), loop endings that hand back to the first line ("All for…").

## What I will not claim
No invented numbers, quotes or causes. Disputed details are dropped or told as "the most popular answer". Nothing gory (cartoon, no blood), nothing made-for-kids.

## My format
- Vertical 1080×1920 Short, 30–45 s, ~100–110 words.
- Hook first: open on the craziest moment, big on-screen title for the first ~2.5 s.
- A cut every ~2 s, yellow word-by-word captions, original music + SFX.
- Loop ending, no fade to black. No face on camera; Hindi/English (Hinglish) creator, English narration.
- Results so far: most Shorts plateau at 1–2K views; best was 1.7K with 67% stayed-to-watch. The current fix being tested: stronger hook, shorter, loop.
