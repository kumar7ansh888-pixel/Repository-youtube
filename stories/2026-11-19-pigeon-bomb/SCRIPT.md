## Script
This bomb was steered… by a pigeon.
Three pigeons, in the nose.
A scientist thought:
pigeons have amazing eyes… so make them pilots!
He showed them a picture of the target.
Peck the picture, get a snack.
Peck faster… more snacks.
One greedy bird pecked ten thousand times… in one sitting.
Then he strapped them inside a bomb.
Each got a tiny cockpit.
A screen shows the target. The pigeon pecks…
and the bomb turns.
Why three birds? So they could vote.
Two against one… the loser got outvoted.
And the military paid for this!
In the demo… the birds nailed it.
But nobody would take him seriously.
Cancelled! Then the Navy brought it back.
Wait, it gets worse.
Decades later… it won a prize.
The Ig Nobel… Peace Prize.
Peace. For a bomb. Flown by pigeons.
And the pilots? Paid in grain.
So next time a pigeon stares at you… remember:
