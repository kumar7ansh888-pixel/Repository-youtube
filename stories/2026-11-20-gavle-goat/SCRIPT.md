## Script
This giant goat gets burned… almost every year.
It's made of straw. Thirteen meters tall.
A Swedish town builds it every Christmas.
And the very first year… somebody burned it.
Guards? Burned.
Fences? Burned.
Soaked in fire retardant? Burned anyway.
One guy rammed it with his Volvo.
An American tourist torched it…
and told police he thought it was a legal tradition.
It was not. He got eighteen days in jail.
Two guys came dressed as Santa… and a gingerbread man.
And shot it… with a flaming arrow.
Someone offered a guard money…
to steal the goat by helicopter.
One year it burned hours after it opened.
So now it has cameras, double fences, and a live webcam.
Wait, it gets worse.
One year… nobody burned it.
birds ate it.
A gang of jackdaws pecked it down to the bones.
Then a storm knocked it over.
And the town still builds it. Every year.
Because somehow…
