# Napoleon vs the Bunnies

## Script
Napoleon conquered half of Europe… then bunnies beat him. July 1807. He just made peace with Russia. So his chief of staff, Berthier, plans a party. A rabbit hunt. Hundreds of rabbits wait in cages. But there's one problem. Berthier didn't catch wild rabbits. He bought tame ones… from local farmers. The cages open. The hunters raise their guns. And the rabbits… charge. Straight at Napoleon. They think he brought dinner. They swarm his boots. Climb his legs. His men swing riding crops and sticks. The bunnies keep coming. So the emperor runs… to his carriage. And drives away. Defeated. So remember…
