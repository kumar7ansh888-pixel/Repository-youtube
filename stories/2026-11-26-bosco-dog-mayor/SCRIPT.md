## Script
A dog won an election… against two humans.
His name? Bosco.
Half Labrador, half Rottweiler… full politician.
His town wanted an honorary mayor.
Someone nominated Bosco… as a joke.
Big mistake. For the humans.
His campaign promise?
A bone in every dish…
a cat in every tree…
and a fire hydrant on every corner.
The humans had speeches.
Bosco had a tail.
Bosco wins. The humans lose… to a dog.
Mayor Bosco gets straight to work.
Patrolling Main Street.
Greeting voters.
Hanging out in front of the bar.
Bribes? Only belly rubs.
Wait, it gets weirder.
China's official newspaper finds out…
and writes an editorial about him.
Proof, it says, that American democracy has gone to the dogs.
Bosco? Totally unfazed.
He stays mayor for thirteen years…
until the day he dies.
Today, he has his own bronze statue.
Because once upon a time…

## Sources
- Wikipedia, "Bosco Ramos": black Labrador Retriever / Rottweiler mix; elected honorary mayor of Sunol in 1981, defeating two humans; served until his death in 1994; in 1990 the People's Daily reported on his tenure as an alleged example of the failings of the American electoral process; bronze statue in front of the post office, 2008.
- Local News Matters (Bay City News), "How a dog, Bosco Ramos, became the most loved mayor of Sunol in the 80s" (14 Dec 2020): Lab–Rottweiler mix, 1981, beat two human opponents; platform "a bone in every dish, a cat in every tree, and a fire hydrant on every corner"; seen hanging out in front of the local bar; 13 years until he died in 1994; statue unveiled 19 Dec 2008 in front of the post office.
- Deseret News / AP, "Mayor dog sweet despite sour China media" (25 Feb 1990): People's Daily front-page editorial said his election "proves that American democracy is going to the dogs"; Bosco "unfazed"; patrols Main Street; slogan "a bone in every dish, a cat in every tree and fireplug at every corner".
- Atlas Obscura, "Bosco the Dog Mayor": nominated as a joke, beat two actual humans, held office 1981 until his death in 1994; bronze statue outside the post office (2008).
- Kept out: the pro-democracy rally at the Chinese consulate (single source, and political), the puppies (single source), the 75-of-120 vote count (single source), the "honorary mayor" office details beyond "honorary".
