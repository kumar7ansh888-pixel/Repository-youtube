# This Penguin Is a General in the Army 🐧

Topic: Sir Nils Olav, the king penguin at Edinburgh Zoo who is the mascot of the Norwegian King's Guard: adopted in 1972 as a lance
corporal, promoted every time the Guard returns, knighted in 2008 (approved by King Harald V), Brigadier 2016, Major General 2023
(third-highest rank in the Norwegian Army), three penguins have held the name, bronze statues in Edinburgh and at Huseby (Oslo),
and in 2026 he became a father.

## Script
This penguin outranks almost an entire army.
He's a general. A real one.
With a knighthood.
It starts when Norway's royal guard visits a Scottish zoo.
The soldiers fall in love… with a penguin.
So they adopt him. His name? Nils Olav.
Rank: lance corporal.
Every time the guard comes back… he gets promoted.
Corporal. Sergeant. Sergeant major.
He doesn't march. He waddles.
Then the King of Norway approves… a knighthood.
"In every way qualified," says the royal citation.
Rows of soldiers stand at attention… while a penguin inspects them.
Brigadier. Major general.
Now he outranks the man he was named after.
But wait… it gets weirder.
The first Nils died years ago.
A lookalike took his place… and kept getting promoted.
Today it's Nils the Third. He even has a bronze statue.
And he just became a dad.
So somebody… start the paperwork.
Because in this army…
