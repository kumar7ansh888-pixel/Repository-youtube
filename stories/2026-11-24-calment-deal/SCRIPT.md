HE BET SHE'D|DIE SOON…

## Script
He bet an old lady would die soon.
Big mistake. Huge.
She's ninety. He's a lawyer, half her age.
The deal? He pays her every single month…
and when she dies, her apartment is his.
Easy money, right? A few years, tops.
Years pass. She's still here.
More years. She's riding her bicycle… at a hundred.
She smokes. Some weeks, she eats a kilo of chocolate.
Every month, he pays. Every month, she's fine.
Decades later… he's still paying.
He gets old… she just gets older.
Wait, it gets worse.
The lawyer… dies first.
His family? They have to keep paying.
And she just keeps cashing the checks.
She becomes the oldest person who ever lived.
In the end, they paid more than double what the apartment was worth.
He never even got the keys.
Her verdict? "In life, one sometimes makes bad deals."
He sure did. He bet…

## Sources
- Guinness World Records, "Oldest ever person's genius deal with lawyer meant she got paid to live in her own home" (21 Feb 2025): at 90, in 1965, she sold her Arles apartment to lawyer André-François Raffray on condition she could live there for the rest of her life; 2,500 francs a month; he died in 1995 aged 77 (she was 120); his family continued to pay; he ended up paying more than double the value of the apartment; "In life, one sometimes makes bad deals."
- Guinness World Records, "Oldest person ever: 122-year-old Jeanne Calment's extraordinary life" (21 Feb 2024): Raffray then aged 47; family required to keep paying; payments over double the apartment's value; cycled until 100; smoked until 117; sometimes a kilogram of chocolate a week.
- Wikipedia, "Jeanne Calment": 1965, aged 90, life-estate contract with Raffray, 2,500 francs a month; he died 25 Dec 1995, his family had to continue; more than double the apartment's value; "in life, one sometimes makes bad deals" (NYT/AP 1995); cycling until her 100th birthday; smoking until 117; chocolate up to a kilogram a week; died 4 Aug 1997 at 122, oldest verified person.
