# He Ate a Whole Airplane

Hook title: HE ATE A|WHOLE PLANE?!

## Script
A whole airplane… this man ate it.
Not a toy. A real plane. Guinness says so.
He sawed it up… into bite-sized chunks…
drank mineral oil… and swallowed every piece whole.
It took him about two years.
His stage name meant… Mister… Eat… All.
And he meant it.
For starters? Bicycles. Lots of bicycles.
Main course? Shopping carts.
Dessert? A few TV sets. Yum… crunchy.
Plus a pair of skis… a chandelier…
and a coffin. Handles included.
Doctors checked him out… totally confused.
He had a real condition called pica.
A craving for things that are not food.
But wait, it gets worse.
Guinness gave him a record for the strangest diet…
and a shiny brass plaque.
So… he ate the plaque too.
Please… do not try this at home. Seriously.
Now the craziest part.
Bananas made him sick.
Hard-boiled eggs? Also sick.
But you know what didn't?

## Facts (checked)
- Michel Lotito (1950–2007), French performer "Monsieur Mangetout" (= Mister Eats-All / Mr Eat All). Per Guinness World Records he ate a Cessna 150 light
  aircraft piece by piece between 1978 and 1980 (about two years); cut objects into bite-sized chunks (Guinness: with an electric power saw), swallowed them
  whole, drank mineral oil and lots of water (Guinness World Records 2022 article; Wikipedia "Michel Lotito"; Today I Found Out).
- Also ate 18 bicycles, 15 shopping carts/supermarket trolleys, 7 TV sets, 6 chandeliers, 2 beds, a pair of skis, a computer, a coffin with its handles
  (Guinness; Wikipedia; Today I Found Out).
- Pica; doctors found no medical explanation (Guinness: biopsies and tests normal) (Guinness; Wikipedia).
- Guinness awarded him a brass plaque for the strangest diet and he ate it (Guinness; Wikipedia).
- Bananas and hard-boiled eggs made him sick (Guinness: nausea/indigestion; Today I Found Out: "hard-boiled eggs and bananas made him sick"; Wikipedia: bananas).
- Caveat: Snopes (2022) could not independently verify the airplane, so the script attributes it ("Guinness says so"). Wikipedia gives his death as
  2006 vs Guinness 2007; not used on screen.
