# He Won Olympic Gold on Rat Poison

## Script
He won Olympic gold… on rat poison. The worst marathon ever. Boiling heat. Choking dust. A runner gets cramps… so he hops in a car. He waves at runners as he drives past. The car breaks down, so he jogs in. The President's daughter crowns him. Then somebody snitches. Meanwhile, a Cuban mailman eats rotten apples… and takes a nap. But the real winner? His coaches kept feeding him brandy, egg… and strychnine. Rat poison. Hallucinating, held up by his coaches… he wins. So yes…

## Sources (checked 2026-11-14)
- Wikipedia, "Athletics at the 1904 Summer Olympics – Men's marathon": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1904_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_marathon — ~90 °F heat, dust from officials' cars, the only water a well at about halfway; Fred Lorz dropped out with cramps after nine miles, rode in a car, waved at spectators and runners, car broke down, jogged in first, Alice Roosevelt placed a wreath on his head before spectators objected, banned (later reduced); Thomas Hicks given strychnine with brandy and egg white, hallucinating, held up by his support team, declared winner; Andarín Carvajal, a Cuban postman, ate rotten apples from an orchard, lay down for a nap, finished fourth.
- TheCollector, "Why Did the 1904 Olympic Marathon End in Disaster?": https://www.thecollector.com/1904-olympic-marathon-disaster/ — Lorz hitched a ride in a vehicle about nine miles in and "waved at other contestants and people lined up to watch"; Hicks given "brandy, raw egg, and 1.1 mg of strychnine", hallucinating; Carvajal ate bad apples off a tree and took a nap.
- Strychnine = a rat poison (pesticide) and a stimulant then; script says "egg" (sources differ: egg white / raw egg).
- Kept out: exact miles, the temperature figure, Lorz's "joke" defence, Hicks' weight loss.
