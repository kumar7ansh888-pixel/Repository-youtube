# Mike the Headless Chicken

## Script
A chicken that lived eighteen months… without a head. Colorado, 1945. Farmer Lloyd Olsen swings his axe for dinner. But the chicken gets up… and walks away. His name was Mike. The axe missed the part of his brain that keeps a chicken alive. A blood clot stopped the bleeding. So Lloyd fed him with an eyedropper… straight down the neck. Mike still tried to peck. Tried to crow. With no head. Soon Lloyd took him on tour. Twenty-five cents a look. Time and Life magazines came for photos. Then one night, in a Phoenix motel… Mike choked. And it was over. But today, his town still throws a festival every year. All for…
