## Script
A man flew a lawn chair… into the sky.
Not a plane. A lawn chair.
He dreamed of being a pilot. Bad eyesight said no.
So he bought a pile of giant weather balloons… and filled them with helium.
He packed sandwiches. And beer.
And the brakes? A pellet gun… to pop the balloons.
The plan: float gently over the neighborhood.
Then the rope snapped.
He shot up like a cork… sixteen thousand feet.
Two airliners spotted him. A guy… in a lawn chair.
So he started shooting balloons. Pop. Pop. Pop.
He was finally coming down! Then… oops. He dropped the gun.
Wait, it gets worse.
He drifted down… straight into power lines.
A whole neighborhood blacked out.
