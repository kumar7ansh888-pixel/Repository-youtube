## Facts (checked)
- Escobar's private zoo at Hacienda Nápoles held exotic animals incl. giraffes and zebras; he had 4 hippos (1 male, 3 females), brought illegally in
  the 1980s (CNN 2023-11-03; NPR 2026-05-08; Wikipedia "Hacienda Nápoles").
- After his death (1993) authorities moved the other animals; the hippos were too difficult to seize/transport and were left (CNN; Wikipedia).
- They escaped into lakes and the Magdalena River basin; ~200 today (NPR 2026: "around 200"; CNN 2023: 169; Wikipedia 2023: 166).
  Locals/media call them "cocaine hippos" (NatGeo documentary "Cocaine Hippos"; NPR headline).
- Birth control (contraceptive darts/injections) and castrations (4 males castrated, 2 females sterilized 2011-2019) barely slowed growth (CNN; NPR).
- Male hippos' testes are "spatially dynamic" and can retract into the inguinal canal; one 2013 team tracked a male for three months, used a potent
  elephant tranquilliser, and found it "surprisingly difficult to locate his parts"; the vet (Echeverri López) can castrate roughly one hippo a year
  (Daily Maverick 2021-01-17; The Conversation "Moving testicles frustrate effort to calm hippos by castration").
- Hippos spray dung by spinning their tails ("dung showering"), general hippo behaviour (Live Science "Hippos unleash poop tornado…"; San Diego Zoo Global hippo fact sheet).
- Anant Ambani's Vantara reserve in Gujarat, India offered to take up to 80 hippos, flown out from Rionegro airport; Colombia is reviewing the offer
  (NPR 2026-05-08; OPB/WBAA syndication). Script says only "offered to take some… By plane."
