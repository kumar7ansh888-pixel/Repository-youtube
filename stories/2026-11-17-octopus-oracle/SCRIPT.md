# The Octopus That Predicted the World Cup

Hook title: OCTOPUS PREDICTED|THE WORLD CUP?!

## Script
An octopus predicted the World Cup… perfectly.
His name was Paul. He lived in a tank.
Each game, his keepers lowered two boxes.
Each box had a mussel… and a team's flag.
Whichever box he opened first… would win.
Paul picked Germany. Germany won.
Then he picked Serbia… Germany lost!
TV crews came to watch an octopus eat lunch.
Then the semifinal. Germany against Spain.
Paul… picked Spain.
Spain won. And German fans turned on him.
People suggested frying him… or feeding him to sharks.
Wait, it gets worse. Spain's Prime Minister got involved.
He said he was worried… and thought about sending him protection.
Then Paul picked Spain to win the final.
Spain won. Eight out of eight.

To star at their octopus festival. Where people eat octopus.

## Facts (checked)
- Paul (hatched 2008 at Sea Life Weymouth, England; lived at Sea Life Oberhausen, Germany). Keepers showed him two clear boxes, each with food
  (a mussel or oyster) and a team flag; the box he opened/ate from first was the pick (Wikipedia "Paul the Octopus"; Fox News; CS Monitor).
- 2010 World Cup: 8 of 8 correct: Germany's seven matches (incl. picking Serbia, who beat Germany, and Spain in the semifinal) and the final, Spain
  (Wikipedia; Herald/AP, LJWorld).
- After the semifinal pick, people proposed frying, grilling or cooking him, or feeding him to sharks; fans sang anti-octopus songs (Soccer America /
  dpa; Wikipedia: German fans called for him to be eaten).
- PM Zapatero: "I am concerned for the octopus", "thinking of sending him a protective team"; minister Miguel Sebastián offered a free transfer
  to Spain (Soccer America; Wikipedia).
- Spanish businessmen offered €30,000 to bring him to the octopus festival (Fiesta del Pulpo) in O Carballiño; Sea Life said no (Wikipedia;
  TimesLive/Sapa 2010-07-10; Fox News: aquarium rejects Madrid bid).
- Not used: his death in Oct 2010 (natural, ~2.5 years old).
