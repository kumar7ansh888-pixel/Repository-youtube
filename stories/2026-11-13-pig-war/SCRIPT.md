# The Pig War

## Script
The only casualty of this war… was a pig. June 1859. A tiny island America and Britain both claim. Farmer Lyman Cutlar finds a pig eating his potatoes. He shoots it. Problem… the pig is British. Cutlar offers ten dollars. The owner wants a hundred. Then America sends four hundred sixty-one soldiers… and fourteen cannons. Britain sends five warships… and over two thousand troops. But the British admiral won't start a war… over a pig. So both armies camp there… for twelve years. Until Germany's Kaiser settles it. America gets the island. Nobody fires a shot. So when the war finally ends…
