This policy explains what information the Udan Coaching app ("app", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. The app is run by [LEGAL NAME OF THE COACHING / OWNER], [ADDRESS].

Information we collect

  • Mobile number: to log you in with a one time password (OTP).
  • Name, class and city: entered by you, to set up your profile and show the right batches.
  • Purchases: which batch and plan you bought, the amount, date and payment reference. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.
  • Test results: your answers and scores in app tests, so you can review them.
  • Technical data: basic device and log information collected by Google Firebase to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To give you access to the batches you bought and to show your progress.
  • To answer support requests and send important service messages.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party ads.

Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase (login, database, hosting): stores app data on our behalf.
  • Razorpay: processes payments.
  • YouTube / video hosting: lecture videos may be streamed from these services.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, test results and batch access are deleted. Payment records are kept for [8] years because tax law requires it.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in the app (Profile → Edit).
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. If you are under 18, please use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian. Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's information.
